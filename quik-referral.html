← All work Fixing a broken MVP

From a fragile MVP → to a system the founders could control.

Quik Referral had a working product—but no control, clarity or cost predictability. We helped them turn it into a system they could run.

  • Pre-revenue
  • 3 beta users
  • 8 months
  • Product + Engineering Stabilization

About the product

Quik Referral is a referral platform for local businesses, helping them grow through trusted recommendations.

The problem

The product worked. The system didn’t.

  • Codebase spread across multiple repositories
  • No documentation
  • Developers pulling from production
  • No staging or testing workflow
  • Scattered communication
  • Paying for structure that didn’t exist

The risk

It was unsustainable.

Costs kept rising. Progress slowed.
The team stayed dependent—and eventually, everything would stall.

What we did

We didn’t add more. We fixed what mattered.

Ownership

Everything moved to founder-controlled systems. No more agency dependency.

Developer experience

Clear setup. No production hacks. Onboarding a new developer takes hours, not days.

Infrastructure

Staging and production separated. Deployments automated.

Systems

Slack, Linear, Confluence. Clear workflows replaced chaos.

Cost & focus

Removed unnecessary layers. Focused only on high-impact work.

Beyond engineering

GTM, onboarding, positioning. Not just code.

What changed

Before

  • Fragile MVP
  • Unclear direction
  • High burn
  • No ownership
  • Reactive chaos
→

After

  • Stable system
  • Clear workflows
  • Predictable costs
  • Internal team enabled
  • Full ownership
  1. Month 1 Stabilization & cleanup
  2. Month 3 Systems & workflows in place
  3. Month 8 Stable, scalable product

The shift

This wasn’t a technical fix. It was an operational shift.

From reacting to problems → to running a structured product system.
